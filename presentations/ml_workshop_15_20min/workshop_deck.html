SYNTHESIS

Dendrites provide conditional resources for local credit

1 · identify the neuron

One learning coordinate per neuron carries most of the practical benefit on ordinary tasks.

2 · address the subtree

Useful when branches require different task-dependent updates and route bandwidth is matched.

3 · regulate route gain

Conductance can shape transport, but only in an appropriate electrotonic state.

The decisive variable is alignment between task credit and the available route span.
Dendritic structure is a conditional substrate for routing local credit—not a general replacement for backpropagation.
