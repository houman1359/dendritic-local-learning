# Timing and cut map

## Recommended 18–20 minute talk

Use all 20 slides. The timed notes total approximately 18.5 minutes, leaving a
small transition buffer.

## ML-focused 17-minute cut

Use slides:

`1–15, 17–20`

Skip slide 16, the forward physical-depth experiment. The story then remains
focused on backward credit routing while retaining anatomy, shunting, and the
measured-response boundary.

## Tight 15-minute cut

Use slides:

`1–8, 10–13, 17–20`

Skip the explicit roadmap (9), the second controlled hierarchy experiment
(14–15), and forward physical depth (16). This cut preserves:

1. the motivation and exact derivation;
2. the standard-task boundary;
3. the credit-operator prediction;
4. one fully defined necessity task and its result;
5. anatomical capacity, conditional shunting, the measured null and rescue;
6. the final conditional claim.

Do not remove slide 12 while retaining slide 13: the branch-conflict result is
not interpretable without its task definition. Likewise, slides 14 and 15 are
a task/result pair.

## If discussion is likely to focus on theory

Use the full sequence through slide 15, then jump to slide 20. This gives about
14 minutes of talk and leaves the biological analyses for questions.
