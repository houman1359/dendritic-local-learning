# Source Data and provenance

This directory holds the numerical record for the standalone Article
*Dendritic morphology as a dictionary for local credit assignment*. Some large
result tables are ignored by the Overleaf-synchronized Git branch; release
archives are assembled from explicit current-file allowlists with source hashes.
The historical `archive/pre-overleaf-prune-20260820` reference is not available
in this checkout and is not assumed to recover missing execution artifacts.

`provenance_manifest.tsv` maps stable historical entry IDs to current figures,
panels, source paths, generators and replication units. Figure assets and
numerical sources are separate records. The final updater enforces the current
layout before refreshing hashes. `inferential_units.csv` summarizes sample
units. Sites, scans, stimulus splits and candidate models remain nested or
paired observations, not independent biological or training replicates.

## Current publication map

The main sequence is: 1 framework and image learning; 2 contextual branch selection;
3 ancestry at matched bandwidth; 4 interaction order and learned credit geometry;
5 context-dependent credit in conductance trees; 6 physical depth and training budget;
7 anatomy beyond broadcast; 8 focal shunting as ancestry gain; and 9 measured responses.
The supplement retains the complete earlier evidence, the normalized shunt dose
response, and the new image and conductance controls. Current figure counts and
exact source assignments are enforced by the release inventory.
The six-animal signed-coordinate reanalysis is supplementary context only.

## Supporting investigations

- `conductance_credit_demand`: separately frozen monotonic and opponent-tuning studies, complete development and fresh cohorts, extended training, parameter-range sensitivity, an equally budgeted expanded-rate screen, and common-state gradient diagnostics.
- `image_ladder_controls`: trained one- and three-pattern dictionaries, matched learning-rate selection, frozen-core decoder references, coordinate-specific capture, and portable cohort-specific runtime records.
- `review_evidence_reanalysis`: deterministic/global-curvature utility, explicit noise comparison, paired ancestry controls, native-unit functional effects and retrospective prediction limits.
- `fulltree_within_span_oracle`: common-state replay geometry, with numerical drift retained; no oracle-trained performance.
- `review_curve_lineage`: bounded source/run validity audit. Legacy S1/S3 shunting execution remains unresolved; S8 uses the retained run-level validity classification.
- `prospective_morphology_selection`: frozen development and held-out task/seed split,12,800 confirmatory candidate fits and sealed choices. Local utility predicts first-step improvement but loses to strong long-horizon baselines. All data and posthoc diagnostics remain separate and labeled.
- `review_coefficient_encoder` and `review_coefficient_hard_readout`: learned trial-dependent route coefficients from imposed local cues, including their oracle gap and delayed/noisy-cue limits.
- `review_branch_trajectories`: full branch-conflict gradient trajectories and exact endpoint replay; public Fashion-MNIST cache checksums are retained.
- `review_morphology_uncertainty`: direct/proxy labels, missingness and mapping/compression sensitivity in eight cells from one mouse.
- `review_response_baselines`: nested ridge, mean and reliability-filtered baselines plus input missingness/noise sensitivity on 13 scans in seven targets.

Original evidence tables and removed duplicate-panel sources remain archived;
the release manifest identifies their current display or background role.
Raw restricted/upstream caches and credentials are excluded. Source identifiers,
public file URLs and hashes provide the relevant provenance without claiming
missing raw execution metadata is available.

## Updating and packaging

Run `python scripts/update_provenance_hashes.py` after the final analysis and
figure files are frozen, then the strict manuscript/SI audits and release
builders. Do not silently replace a frozen source while retaining its old hash.
The Source Data builder copies only allowed files and preserves its explicit
private-path scrubbing. A valid archive checksum does not by itself establish
that the archive matches a newer manuscript revision.


The morphology studies comprise `morphology_structure` (exact finite-domain diagnostics and tree/depth construction), `morphology_finite_horizon` (frozen dynamic forecasts and fresh fits), `morphology_calibration` (independent noisy selection and an additional end-to-end gradient-learning bridge), `morphology_credit` (oracle-compatible trees learned under five credit rules), and `morphology_conductance` (positive-conductance grouping, feedback controls and an analytic interaction bound). Their README, protocol, selection seal and numerical validation files specify access to labels, calibration, target-informed references and the comparison unit. The prospective initialization-selector result and the distinct structure/credit studies have separate destinations in the current supplementary contents. The current panel manifest supplies their figure numbers and preserves original source identities.

The Boolean extension adds `boolean_theory` (105 exhaustive task–tree cases, 49 exact constructions, canonical derivatives and separately labeled post hoc target projections) and `boolean_morphology` (6,720 fresh fits, 1,680 development fits and 336 excluded smoke fits). S43–S44 retain complete theory, parameter learning, all rates, classification controls and independent audits. The primary grouping effect passes, whereas the small aligned XOR-of-AND credit effect fails its predeclared 0.01 practical margin; parity credit effects are descriptive. Seven fixed logical templates are repeated across seeds, not sampled as new task families. These cohorts are separate from the earlier morphology bridges.
