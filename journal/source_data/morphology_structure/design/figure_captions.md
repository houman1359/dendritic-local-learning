# Research figure captions

These are exploratory research artifacts. They have not been inserted into the journal manuscript or assigned manuscript figure numbers.

## morphology_structure_diagnostic

**Interaction structure distinguishes fixed-resource scalar trees even when the complete input-sensitivity spectrum is identical.** **A**, Exhaustive target families comprise all 105 perfect matchings and all 35 unordered four-plus-four partitions of eight independent Rademacher inputs. Both have input-gradient second moment I₈/4 and participation-ratio effective rank 8. Target variances are 1 and 1/2, respectively; error is divided by the corresponding variance. Each candidate has eight named input leaves, seven scalar multi-affine internal units, 28 coefficients and 14 edges, with a root-only output. **B**, Candidate-specific lower bounds from the full Fourier-matrix rank-two constraint (left) and the stronger rank-one constraint after removing its constant-subtree row (right), versus the best population NMSE from four fixed ALS restarts. The bound is the maximum squared singular-value tail over actual subtree cuts. Points aggregate coincident task–candidate endpoints; marker size indicates multiplicity. The dashed line is equality. **C**, Mean achieved NMSE for three shapes, each tested at the same four input assignments. White diamonds mark mean centered-cut lower bounds. **D**, Mean excess NMSE above the best fitted candidate among the fixed twelve candidates. Deterministic score ties use absolute tolerance 10⁻¹⁰ and then lexicographic candidate ID. The sum of centered tails is a heuristic, not an error bound. The fitting pilot uses two ALS sweeps per candidate; the best-fixed baseline uses each family's outcomes in hindsight; random expectation averages uniformly over candidates. All fits evaluate all 256 input patterns, with four restarts and 32 ALS sweeps. Family averages are exhaustive finite-family quantities, so no sampling confidence intervals are drawn. A positive fit-to-bound gap may reflect optimization error, bound looseness or both. These bounds are specific to nodes affine in each child; they are not established for general nonlinear or conductance trees.

## morphology_constructive_diagnostic

**Exact-target construction identifies compatible input grouping and minimum depth within the multi-affine scalar-tree model.** **A,B**, Actual trees returned by the corrected dynamic program for the first enumerated matching target and the first seeded nested-prefix control. Square leaves have access only to the indicated input; circular nodes implement u=a+bℓ+cr+dℓr, and only the root is read out. These examples are selected by enumeration order, not by reconstruction quality. **C**, Minimum achievable maximum centered-cut bound over all labeled binary trees subject to the indicated depth limit, computed independently for every target. The curves coincide within each family. For all 24 nested-prefix controls, every tree of depth at most three has an NMSE lower bound of 0.146446609407; a zero-bound tree exists at depth four. Both primary families already admit zero-bound trees at depth three. The centered-rank conditions are necessary and sufficient for exact representation within this model, as proved in the accompanying design audit. **D**, Exhaustive reconstruction counts and minimum exact depths. Every construction has 28 coefficients and 14 edges. Dominant centered Fourier directions define local scalar features, and local least squares recovers their four coefficients. All 164 reconstructions have NMSE below 7×10⁻³⁰ and maximum absolute coefficient equal to one up to numerical roundoff. The primary families have the same isotropic input-sensitivity spectrum. The separate nested-prefix family has rank eight but a different, anisotropic spectrum that is constant across its 24 unique input permutations; it is a shape/depth positive control, not an additional isotropic primary family. The algorithm uses the complete target Fourier tensor from all 256 inputs. It is a representational construction with full target access, not a prospective prediction from limited calibration data or a general biological morphology rule.

## Reproduction

From the journal directory, run:

```bash
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/morphology_structure/audit_analysis.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/morphology_structure/plot_results.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python scripts/morphology_structure/plot_constructive.py
```

These commands read retained population fits and constructive results. They run no new training. The final command recomputes the depth-constrained Fourier dynamic program and writes its complete 492-row certificate table.
