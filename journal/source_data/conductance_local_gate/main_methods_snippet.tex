The local-gate follow-up preserves the seven-compartment model and all 24 log-conductance parameters. Student terminal gains are nominally one; proximal inhibition, daughter couplings and somatic couplings share the teacher's nominal scaffold, with independent Gaussian SD~0.4 log perturbations. Teacher perturbations have SD~0.15. The hard rule gates only terminal errors using the parent's supplied inhibitory activity; proximal and somatic errors remain shared. Reversed selection and selection applied to proximal credit provide controls. The latter makes the two proximal inhibitory-gain gradients identically zero. A continuous local control uses $[1+g_{\mathrm I,p}a_p]^{-1}$. Two weighted leaf-profile columns also receive oracle projection coefficients while proximal credit remains one; this is not a two-column dictionary over all six nonsomatic sites.

The committed protocol records prior exposure to exploratory gate results, which are excluded from the fresh cohort. Twenty new paired seed blocks cross two tasks, nine rules and three frozen Adam rates, 0.01, 0.03 and 0.1, giving 1,080 trajectories. Rate 0.03 and the 4,096-update window define the primary comparisons. Every fit continues uninterrupted to 16,384 updates; validation selects checkpoints within each window, with fixed-step endpoints retained. Inputs, initialization and minibatches are paired. Paired intervals bootstrap seed blocks 20,000 times. Two exact sign-flip tests cover the opposed-task and task-by-rule contrasts, with Holm correction; remaining comparisons are descriptive. Full data sizes, optimizer settings, bounds, eligibility tests and excluded historical replay appear in Supplementary Section~S4.
