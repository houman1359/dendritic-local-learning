# Prospective local-gate comparison

This follow-up was specified after exposure to a referee panel's exploratory local-gating results. Those outcomes are prior evidence, not part of the reported cohort. The protocol and independent adapter were committed before scientific execution. Twenty new seed blocks, 2026090801–2026090820, share teacher perturbations, initial parameters, features, contexts and minibatch streams across the paired aligned/opposed tasks and all credit rules. The held-out targets differ across task families. A separate implementation seed and historical replay are excluded from scientific inference.

The model preserves the directed seven-compartment conductance circuit: four leaves feed two proximal compartments and a soma. Sixteen terminal E/I log gains, two proximal inhibitory log gains, four daughter-coupling log gains and two somatic-coupling log gains are represented in every condition. Inputs are the same positive exp(±z) feature pairs in both subtrees, with two independent z drawn uniformly from [−2,2]. Binary context supplies inhibitory activity a_p=10 to one proximal compartment and zero to the other. Teacher branches have either aligned or opposing feature tuning. Student terminal gains have nominal value one; other nominal conductances share the teacher scaffold (proximal inhibition 15, daughter couplings 20/0.2, soma couplings 4), with independently drawn SD 0.4 log perturbations. Teacher log perturbations have SD 0.15. Students do not inherit the teacher's exact random realization.

All updates combine the somatic error δ=(V_s−y)/Var_train(y) with the same local derivative of compartment voltage with respect to its log conductance. The primary local rule multiplies every terminal's error by 1[a_parent=0], while retaining unit error multipliers at both proximal compartments and the soma. Thus the distal teaching signal is δ times a locally supplied context gate; it evaluates neither exact path derivatives nor a projection of the exact field during learning. The inhibitory context is imposed externally, and the rule does not discover that cue or generate the somatic error biologically.

Nine rules are retained. Exact, uniform, initially calibrated and three-pattern oracle rules reproduce the earlier comparators. The calibrated pattern averages exact initial derivatives over 512 separate unlabeled examples. Three-pattern projection has two weighted terminal-subtree columns and one weighted proximal-pair column, with coefficients derived from the current exact field. The new two-leaf-pattern control retains only the two weighted distal projection columns and sets proximal multipliers to one. It is therefore a two-pattern distal oracle plus a fixed common proximal rule, not an entirely two-dimensional six-site dictionary or a learned coefficient encoder. Additional controls reverse the local gate, or apply it to the proximal compartments as well. The latter is intentionally flawed: proximal inhibitory eligibility is nonzero only when inhibitory activity is present, whereas the hard proximal gate is then zero, making the two inhibitory-gain gradients identically zero. A secondary local rule uses the continuous distal factor 1/(1+g_I,parent a_parent), keeping proximal and somatic multipliers one. It omits downstream soma derivatives and daughter-coupling corrections.

Each task/seed supplies 2,048 training,1,024 validation,4,096 test,512 calibration and 1,024 diagnostic examples. No label noise is added. Adam uses β1=0.9,β2=0.999,ε=10⁻8, no weight decay, batches of 128, global gradient clipping at norm 10, and log-conductance bounds[−7,7]. The primary learning rate 0.03 is fixed from the previous protocol. Rates 0.01 and 0.1 are symmetrical robustness conditions for every rule and seed; fresh outcomes never select a new primary rate. The 20×2×9×3 design gives 1,080 initialized trajectories. Every trajectory continues uninterrupted from the primary 4,096-update budget through 16,384 updates. Minimum validation NMSE within each budget selects the reported test checkpoint; fixed-budget endpoint values and every saved curve point are also retained. These are finite-budget comparisons, not convergence claims.

Primary estimands at rate 0.03 and the 4,096-update window are the opposed-task broadcast-minus-local-gate NMSE and the difference in that gap between opposed and aligned targets. Inference resamples whole paired seed blocks,20,000 times, for percentile 95% intervals. Two exact two-sided sign-flip tests are Holm-corrected across the two primary contrasts. Other rates, budgets, rule contrasts and practical precision checks are descriptive. The prespecified precision reference requires a hard-minus-exact upper 95% interval below 0.001 NMSE and every opposed local-gate seed below 0.01; satisfying it does not establish asymptotic equivalence. All seed values, conductance-bound contacts, parameter/initialization/input hashes, resolved configurations, checkpoints and exclusions are retained.

Ten mechanism tests verify the exact derivatives, reference agreement, absence of exact-path access in local gradient code, preservation of proximal eligibilities, and the proximal-gating failure mechanism. An excluded 4,096-update replay on historical seed 2101 reproduces exact/unit/calibrated trajectories bit for bit; independent three-pattern projection agrees to floating-point association error. The committed prospective protocol, provenance and excluded validation reports accompany the Source Data.
