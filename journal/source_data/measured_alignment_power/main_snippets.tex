% Main Results suggestion; no fixed supplementary figure number.
A response-level sensitivity simulation preserved the observed partners, shared inputs, repeated stimuli and seven-target inference while calibrating noise to measured repeat reliabilities. Under the specified Gaussian ancestry-signal model, 80\% detection corresponded to a mean simulated target-level partial-rank effect of approximately 0.25 (Supplementary Fig.~\ref{fig:supp_measured_alignment_power}). This conditional estimate does not exclude smaller effects or alignment involving unobserved partners.

% Main Methods suggestion.
Conditional sensitivity was assessed in 4,000 simulated complete response datasets preserving the thirteen scans, seven targets, shared presynaptic roots, stimulus identities and repeat counts. Reliable tuning mixed independent and ancestry-correlated Gaussian components; measurement noise was calibrated to observed split-half reliabilities. The original partial-rank statistic and exact seven-target signed-rank test were applied without treating partner pairs as independent. The protocol, complete simulation outcomes and previously unreleased pair-level inputs are provided in Source Data.
