# Conductance implementation of context-dependent credit demand

The experiment tests whether a task that asks the same dendritic tree to express different feature tuning in different contexts benefits from routed credit. It uses the directed steady-state E/I balance of DendriNet, with identity reactivation, rather than a reciprocal cable equation. It does not implement the multi-affine quartic target. The topology contains four terminal compartments, two proximal compartments and one soma. Each proximal compartment receives two terminal daughters. All 24 conductances, including daughter and somatic couplings and context-sensitive inhibitory gains, are plastic.

Let x_j^+=exp(z_j) and x_j^-=exp(-z_j), where z_1,z_2 are independent uniform variables on[-2,2]. Both subtrees receive the same two latent features: terminal compartments 0 and 2 receive z_1; compartments 1 and 3 receive z_2. Each terminal compartment has four positive conductances, g_E+,g_E-,g_I+,g_I-. Its effective excitatory and inhibitory conductances are E=g_E+x^++g_E-x^- and I=g_I+x^++g_I-x^-, and its voltage is v=E/(1+E+I). The normalization fixes leak conductance to 1 and excitatory, inhibitory and leak reversal potentials to 1,0,0. A binary context c is independently sampled with equal probability. Proximal compartment p receives externally supplied inhibitory activity a_p=A 1[c≠p], with A=10 in the primary task pair. Its voltage is v_p=(Σ_j g_pjv_j)/(1+Σ_jg_pj+g_Ip a_p). Somatic voltage is v_s=(g_s 0 v_0+g_s 1 v_1)/(1+g_s 0+g_s 1). Context does not enter the broadcast learning rule.

The target is the somatic response of a teacher with this exact topology and equation, giving a constructive clean forward floor of zero NMSE. In the aligned task all four teacher terminals have nominal gains(8,.25,.25,8), ordered as(E+,E-,I+,I-). In the opposed task terminals 2 and 3 instead have(.25,8,8,.25), reversing their feature tuning. Both tasks use identical positive input features and contexts; their target functions differ. This does not assert equal task input-sensitivity spectra. Teacher log conductances receive independent Gaussian perturbations with standard deviation 0.15, using the same perturbations across the paired tasks. Parent inhibitory gains are nominally 15, daughter couplings are(20,.2) in each subtree, and somatic couplings are 4. Student terminal gains are nominally 1; their other nominal gains match these values. Every one of the 24 student log gains receives an independent Gaussian perturbation of standard deviation 0.4. Generic student initializations, input samples and minibatches are identical across paired task and credit-rule conditions. No exact symmetric initialization is imposed.

Training minimizes one half of mean squared prediction error divided by training-target variance. The somatic error factor is δ=(v_s−y)/Var_train(y). The exact path factor q_j=∂v_s/∂v_j for each of the six non-somatic compartments multiplies its exact local eligibility e_j=∂v_j/∂θ_j, where θ_j is a log conductance. Local eligibility retains the excitatory or inhibitory driving force and denominator. For example, a local excitatory eligibility is(1−v_j)g_E+x^+/D_j, and an inhibitory eligibility is−v_jg_I+x^+/D_j, with D_j=1+E_j+I_j. The eligibility of a daughter coupling into a parent is(v_child−v_parent)g_child/D_parent. Somatic couplings receive the exact somatic factor 1 in every condition. Thus the intervention changes delivered within-tree credit while retaining the forward circuit, local eligibilities, parameters and data.

The four rules deliver the current exact q, a unit profile, a fixed initial profile, or an explicit oracle projection into three fixed spatial patterns. The initial profile p is the mean exact q on 512 independently sampled, unlabeled calibration examples at initialization. The three columns of D retain p on terminal indices(0,1), on terminal indices(2,3), and on proximal indices(4,5), respectively, and are zero elsewhere. The oracle delivers D(DᵀD)⁻¹Dᵀq for each current example. Its coefficients use the current exact field; it is a routing upper bound rather than a locally learned encoder or a claim that three independent external errors are necessary. At fixed parameters, q has only two possible rows because the context is binary. This experiment concerns context-dependent branch selection, not a rank-six or high-order interaction requirement.

There are 2048 training,1024 validation,4096 test and 1024 diagnostic examples for each teacher/seed and task; the calibration sample is separate. Samples are generated independently across these five roles. Labels contain no added noise. Mean test-target variance over the 20 fresh teachers is 0.024108 for aligned tuning and 0.014691 for opposed tuning, with ranges 0.022232–0.026008 and 0.013143–0.016814, respectively. Test NMSE divides MSE by that test set's clean target variance.

Development comprised 3 seeds×4 regimes×4 rules×2 optimizers×3 rates=288 fits. All outcomes are retained. The regimes were aligned/strong gating, opposed/strong gating, opposed/moderate gating(A=1), and opposed/ungated(A=0). Before development outcomes, the preferred strong-opposed regime was required to have mean teacher path rank-one capture≤0.90 and mean exact-Adam validation NMSE≤0.02; a declared fallback order applied if it failed. Between-rule learning gaps were not selection criteria. Strong opposed tuning qualified. A separate selection freeze preceded the 20 fresh paired blocks. Adam rates were 0.003,0.01,0.03 and SGD rates 0.03,0.1,0.3. Each task/rule/optimizer selected its rate by mean development best-validation NMSE with identical tuning budgets. A common rate minimized the mean over rules. For the fresh task pair, every selected rate equals its common rate:0.03 for Adam and 0.3 for SGD. Consequently the selected/common-rate results are the same fits, not independent replications. The fresh cohort has 20×2×4×2=320 fits.

All fits use 128-example minibatches sampled with replacement and 4096 updates. Adam uses β_1=0.9,β_2=0.999 and ε=10⁻⁸; SGD has no momentum. Global gradient norm is capped at 10, and log conductances are projected into[-7,7]. Fresh primary fits and their extensions had no gradient-norm clipping. Parameter projection is a separate intervention: exact and oracle fresh fits never reached these bounds, whereas opposed broadcast fits often did. Validation is evaluated at updates 0,64,256,1024,2048 and 4096, and the saved state with lowest validation NMSE supplies the primary test endpoint. Every fresh fit, including the controls and all rules, was then continued with unchanged optimizer and minibatch state to 16384 updates, with additional validation checkpoints 8192,12288,16384. This extension was declared before fresh outcomes. Its best-validation endpoint includes the earlier saved states. These are stated optimization budgets, not claims of convergence.

The primary paired contrast is(opposed calibrated−exact NMSE)−(aligned calibrated−exact NMSE) at 4096 updates. Its value is 0.663817[0.510621,0.804260] under Adam and 0.748508[0.622532,0.865382] under SGD, positive in all 20 seeds for each optimizer. At the predeclared extended budget these contrasts are 0.627764[0.463911,0.774494] and 0.716191[0.586332,0.838346]. Intervals are paired percentile bootstrap intervals from 20000 resamples of the 20 seed blocks. They describe uncertainty across these generated teachers and initializations. Unit broadcast and three-route oracle contrasts, complete seed values and all validation checkpoints are also supplied.

Post-fit diagnostics compute squared-energy projection captures from the full path field q and the loss-weighted field δq. A best-rank-one oracle uses the largest eigenvalue of FᵀF divided by total field energy, where F is the matrix of examples by compartments. Eligibility-weighted fixed-profile capture minimizes the weighted per-example squared error of q against one freely chosen amplitude times the frozen profile; weights are the sums of squared local parameter eligibilities in each compartment. The loss-eligibility version multiplies those weights by δ². These diagnostics are not training rules. They are reported together because raw spatial-field energy alone does not establish learning necessity.

A common-state gradient audit evaluates every rule on each selected Adam fit's initial,256-update, primary-best and extended-best parameter states, without refitting. For each context it measures the mean distal-parameter update. The cancellation ratio is the norm of the context-probability-weighted sum divided by the probability-weighted sum of context-gradient norms. Lower ratios indicate stronger cancellation when contexts are averaged. All 2560 audited mean gradients exactly reproduce the analytic training adapter. Because all path factors and fixed profiles are positive, their per-example update inner products with exact gradients are nonnegative; the diagnostic concerns conflicting averages, not an incorrect sign for each example.

The original narrow-bound results remain frozen. A separately timestamped posthoc sensitivity restarts every fresh condition from its original initialization with log bounds[-20,20], retains the same rates and 16384-update budget, and replays all original[-7,7] trajectories to record the first parameter-bound contact and verify identity. This produces 640 additional trajectories on the same 20 seed blocks, not 640 independent biological or task replicates. Full outcomes and exact-replay comparisons are reported separately.

The preceding, separately frozen 16-conductance study is retained in full. Its monotonic leaf responses produced a small but reproducible precision benefit: after 16384 updates, the gated-task calibrated−exact gap was 0.000502[0.000267,0.000772] NMSE, with all 20 seeds positive. Under Adam, mean broadcast NMSE remained below0.0006. That study comprised 216 development fits,240 fresh fits and continuation of all 240; it motivated the explicitly separate opponent-tuning development above. Neither its weak outcome nor any unsuccessful development regime was removed.

The moderate-gating development regime has teacher rank-one path capture near0.98 but a substantial broadcast learning deficit. Raw capture is therefore not a universal sufficiency criterion: the smaller spatial component can carry distinctions needed when gradients from different contexts are combined.
