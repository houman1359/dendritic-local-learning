\caption{\textbf{Focal-shunt localization increases with dose under normalized passive parameters.}
The dose is added inhibitory conductance divided by baseline local leak,
excitatory and inhibitory conductance, excluding axial coupling.
A current injection matches the shunt's baseline focal current;
a separately solved somatic current restores baseline somatic voltage
after either intervention. Localization is the descendant minus
depth-matched off-route median absolute log change in
excitatory-conductance gradient magnitude. Positive values indicate a
larger gradient change below the focal intervention. At each dose,
101 focal locations are averaged within eight reconstructed cells;
symbols show equal-weighted cell means and whiskers show descriptive
95\% whole-cell bootstrap intervals from 20,000 draws. Lines connect
the same eight-cell cohort across doses. This normalized passive
comparison complements the signed physical-calibration analysis in
main Fig.~8D and retains the original dose-response outcomes.}
\label{fig:supp_normalized_shunt_dose}
