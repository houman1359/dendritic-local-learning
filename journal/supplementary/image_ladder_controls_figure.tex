\caption{\textbf{Intermediate subtree credit, learning-rate sensitivity and a decoder-only reference on MNIST.} \textbf{A,B}, Development validation cross-entropy at three common multipliers of the original Adam parameter-group rates. Three development seeds per architecture, rule and multiplier give 108 fits; minimum mean validation loss selects each rule's rate. \textbf{C,D}, Test accuracy of ten fresh paired seeds per architecture and rule. Filled circles use selected rates; open squares use the original common rate. Small points retain all seeds; coincident rate conditions reuse one fit. The decoder-only reference trains the linear readout while preserving the initialized core. \textbf{E}, Paired accuracy differences from retaining three profiles instead of one, and from adding exact path resolution to the three-profile dictionary. Both projected rules acquire coefficients from the exact activation-error field, preserving the same common-mode mean at identical parameters; their field norms need not match. Green and blue identify shunting and additive networks. \textbf{F}, Mean field-energy capture by uniform $K=1$ or subtree $K=3$ profiles, before and after exact-credit training. The three-profile dictionary groups each proximal compartment with its three distal children; the one-profile control averages all 12 nonsomatic errors. Capture averages nonzero example--neuron ratios on 2,048 held-out images within each of ten seeds. Voltage-space capture is retained separately in Source Data. Symbols and bars summarize seed means and descriptive 95\% bootstrap intervals. All scientific fits retain the complete MNIST protocol and 180-epoch budget, with validation-based checkpoint selection. Vertical scales differ across panels.}
