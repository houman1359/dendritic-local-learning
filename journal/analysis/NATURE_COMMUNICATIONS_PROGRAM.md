# Nature Communications programme

## Editorial decision

The active target is **Nature Communications, Article**. The project may wait
for the NeurIPS 2026 decision before submission. Until the conference status is
resolved, this directory is a submission-ready working package rather than an
Article under concurrent consideration.

## Central claim

The Article asks what a dendritic tree adds after a circuit has supplied a
neuron-level teaching coordinate:

> Neuronal signals provide signed coordinates, dendritic ancestry supplies
> candidate subtree addresses, conductance regulates route gain, and learning
> improves only when the available routes align with task credit.

This claim is deliberately conditional. The Article does not claim that
MICRONS neurons were observed routing learning signals, that shunting is a
universal optimizer, or that exact path transport is biologically available.

## Narrative spine

1. Backpropagation defines exact parameter credit but is not a literal
   biological mechanism.
2. Point-neuron learning ends at one shared neuronal error coordinate.
3. The conductance-tree derivative factorizes into local eligibility and a
   path-transported compartment error.
4. Prospectively frozen experiments separate coordinate bandwidth, neuron-to-
   tree assignment, path transport and fixed-budget dendritic depth under an
   outcome-independent input-validity rule.
5. A stochastic credit-operator theorem and 50-seed phase experiment show that
   hierarchy depth helps only when retained task signal outweighs omitted
   signal and admitted gradient noise.
6. An exact-resource positive-rate experiment shows that additional physical
   stages help when divisive branch computation is aligned to the task
   hierarchy, under backpropagation and local credit transport.
7. A state-matched positive-conductance model shows that branch-local shunts
   can realize oracle reliability attenuation, with a bounded benefit relative
   to global but not final unshunted learning.
8. Reconstructed arbors define sparse, mainly coarse ancestry routes.
9. Focal shunting can regulate descendant sensitivity in appropriate
   electrotonic regimes.
10. Route capacity improves learning only when it aligns with task gradients.
11. External animal data support signed neuron identity; within-tree transport
   remains the decisive experimental prediction.

## Relationship to the NeurIPS paper

The NeurIPS/arXiv paper is the foundation, not a separate competing story. Its
reusable material comprises the conductance equations, exact
eligibility--error factorization, path-gain recursion, local-learning rule,
regular-tree controls and the visual language of the original figures. The
journal Article cites and discloses this foundation.

The journal conclusions depend on material absent from the conference paper:

- a 640-run prospective depth-by-feedback factorial;
- a 160-run bandwidth-matched neuron-to-tree routing intervention;
- a complete 1,840-run validity ledger, with the signed-input inhibitory-dose
  family excluded and the input-valid fixed-topology/depth boundaries retained;
- a 160-checkpoint credit-to-loss diagnostic;
- stochastic credit-operator and sparse route-dictionary theory;
- a frozen 50-seed spectral, hierarchy-depth, projection and branch-reliability
  phase experiment, plus a secondary reanalysis of all 2,700 factorial fits;
- a fresh 270-fit exact-resource positive-rate physical-depth experiment with
  alignment, shuffled-sensor, reversed-placement, additive and LocalCA controls;
- 200 paired point--dendrite and BP--local-credit controls that separate serial
  composition, point-network capacity, coordinate restriction, optimizer
  effects and path specificity;
- a 90-fit prospective alignment-dose interpolation locating the physical-depth
  boundary without changing the endpoint task;
- a frozen 220-fit literal grouped-point and fresh-H2 extension that replicates
  the serial-composition crossover under BP and both LocalCA transports;
- a preregistered 60-fit Fashion-MNIST replication of the neuron-identity
  bandwidth result;
- a separate 50-seed trained positive-conductance reliability experiment with
  state-matched additive, global, shuffled, anti-aligned and point controls;
- primary and disjoint reconstructed-cell cohorts;
- reciprocal-cable, factor-freeze and physical-calibration controls;
- a synapse-resolved inhibitory-census analysis;
- measured-response alignment boundaries and a controlled sufficiency test;
- an external six-animal signed-coordinate reanalysis; and
- a branch-resolved perturbation prediction.

The submission must include the extension statement and both related
manuscripts. If NeurIPS accepts the paper, the proceedings citation, reuse
license and exact scientific/textual overlap must be updated before journal
submission.

8. focal shunting, active-conductance sensitivity and electrotonic boundary; and

The synapse-resolved inhibitory organization remains a complete ten-panel
display in Supplementary Figure S12. The corrected state-matched
positive-conductance mechanism and its null final no-shunt boundary are
Supplementary Figure S13; the same-span coefficient bias--variance crossover
is Supplementary Figure S14, within the same combined reading PDF.

The inherited NeurIPS visual system remains the style source. Journal figures
may contain more panels, but every panel must advance one of the nine steps
above and remain legible on the canonical 7.2-inch canvas. Six continued
displays carry additional panels with unique consecutive letters, so no
underlying evidence or Source Data was dropped during consolidation.

## Submission threshold

The independent branch-resolved learning experiment and its complete
bandwidth/representation factorial are now complete. No additional broad
artificial-network sweep is required before initial submission. Remaining hard
gates are exclusivity, authorship and declaration
approval, immutable data/code records, current public identifiers, final
Source Data, official forms and a clean-checkout release audit.
