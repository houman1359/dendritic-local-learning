# Frozen irregular-tree wavelet analysis

Date frozen: 19 August 2026

## Question

Do reconstructed, unbalanced MICrONS dendritic trees concentrate the
non-scalar component of conductance-weighted ancestry-route fields at specific
anatomical scales, as predicted by the paper's multiscale address account?

This is a structural capacity analysis. It does not test whether an animal
uses these routes for learning and it does not treat modeled route fields as
measured teaching signals.

## Frozen cohorts

- Primary: the 47-cell public-v661 cohort disjoint from the original pilot.
- Secondary: the original eight reconstructed cells.
- Cells, segment compression, excitatory weights, inhibitory conductances and
  inclusion decisions are inherited without reselection.

## Frozen construction

For each cell, the conductance-weighted ancestry dictionary is

\[
K_{eb}=A_{eb}\,g_b^I/g_b^{\rm tot},
\]

where rows are excitatory segments and columns are inhibitory route roots.
Excitatory synapse area defines the row measure. A deterministic unbalanced
tree-Haar basis recursively splits each current excitatory region at the
descendant subtree whose weight is closest to half of the region weight.
The weighted constant mode is removed before analyzing route-field energy.

Wavelets are grouped by their support fraction of the cell's excitatory
weight:

- coarse: support fraction greater than 1/4;
- intermediate: greater than 1/16 and at most 1/4;
- fine: at most 1/16.

Signal energy is the covariance energy generated by independent unit-variance
route coefficients. Isotropic coordinate noise supplies the analytic
dimension-matched reference. A second null independently permutes each route
column across excitatory sites before weighting and centering; 256 stable,
cell-specific permutations are used.

## Frozen endpoints

1. Primary: within-cell coarse-scale signal enrichment relative to isotropic
   noise in the 47-cell cohort.
2. Primary structural null: actual minus ancestry-permuted coarse energy
   fraction in the 47-cell cohort.
3. Secondary: the same two quantities in the original eight cells.
4. Descriptive: energy and enrichment across all three scales, mode support,
   split balance and the fraction of route energy removed with the scalar mode.

Cell is the inferential unit. Intervals bootstrap cells. Wilcoxon tests are
two-sided and are reported with sign counts; nested permutation draws are not
treated as independent replicates. No threshold is used to suppress a null or
negative result.

## Numerical gates

- exactly one full-rank basis per eligible cell;
- maximum orthonormality error at most `1e-10`;
- maximum actual wavelet-energy decomposition error at most `1e-10`;
- finite scale summaries whose mode counts sum to `n_E - 1`;
- deterministic outputs for a fixed seed and input hashes.

## Claim boundary

A positive result would show that real reconstructed arbors provide a
multiscale structural coordinate system for modeled ancestry-route fields. It
would not show morphology-specific endogenous credit alignment, biological
estimation of route coefficients, or a learning advantage over a
resource-matched point implementation.
