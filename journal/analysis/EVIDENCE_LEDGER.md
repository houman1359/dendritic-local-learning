# Evidence ledger

This ledger links each load-bearing claim to its inferential unit, numerical
result, frozen source and interpretation boundary. It distinguishes completed
evidence from submitted reruns and administrative work.

Status key:

- **Exact**: analytic identity or numerical implementation check.
- **Complete**: analysis finished and source data frozen.
- **Sensitivity**: completed robustness analysis with a stated limitation.
- **Submitted**: compute has been launched but produces no usable evidence
  until validated.
- **Pending**: author, archival or editorial action remains.
