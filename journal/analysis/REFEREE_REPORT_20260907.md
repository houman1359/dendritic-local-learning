# Referee report: "Dendritic morphology as a dictionary for local credit assignment"

Reviewed version: paper repository commit 2a298a9 (7 September 2026), main.pdf 31 pages, Supplementary Information 145 pages with 52 figures and 35 tables. All headline numbers cited below were recomputed by the referee from the supplied Source Data.

## Summary

The manuscript proposes that learning signals delivered across a dendritic arbor can be written as combinations of morphology-constrained spatial patterns (a route dictionary), and asks which spatial distinctions a task requires a learning signal to preserve. A ladder of controlled tasks supports a graded answer: image classification needs only neuron-specific feedback (Fig. 1); context-gated conflict needs branch selection (Fig. 2); hierarchical distractors give a small, design-predicted advantage to ancestry routes at one bandwidth (Fig. 3); pairwise and quartic targets with identical input spectra develop different learned credit spectra and different tolerance of fixed profiles (Fig. 4); a conductance tree with opposed branch tuning under contextual inhibition fails with broadcast credit and succeeds with routed credit (Fig. 5); serial depth helps the forward computation while the earlier credit advantage reverses with longer training (Fig. 6). Reconstructed MICrONS arbors provide ancestry capacity beyond a common broadcast (Fig. 7), a focal shunt acts as a gain on an ancestry partition whose selectivity depends on electrotonic state (Fig. 8), and measured visual responses provide no evidence of ancestry alignment (Fig. 9).

## Overall assessment

This is a substantial, unusually careful and honestly reported computational study. The theory (exact conductance-tree factorization, moment bound, cut-rank criterion, shunt partition identity) is correct. Every number I recomputed reproduces from the Source Data: the MNIST six-rule ladder (9.23 and 7.46 pp; K=3 minus projected K=1 of +0.049 [-0.048, 0.141] and +0.036 [-0.056, 0.122]; decoder-only 80.96 and 89.57 percent), the credit-field capture (0.997 pairwise, 0.461 quartic, 0.448 nested after training; 0.76 for both at initialization), the conductance contrast (0.664 [0.511, 0.804], 20/20 seeds), the physical-depth reversal (+10.86 to -1.52 [-1.83, -1.23], 10/10 seeds; depth gain 38.17), and the anatomical common-mode analysis (0.619 versus 0.511 and 0.578; advantage 0.108 [0.077, 0.135], 45/47 cells). Negative and reversed outcomes are retained and labeled. The release is reproducible from a reachable commit.

The central weakness is that every positive result for spatial credit uses coefficients the neuron cannot compute locally, and the paper does not yet confront this in the place it matters. The second weakness is that the anatomical "independent" field is ancestry-shaped by the paper's own identity. Both are addressable with existing models. I recommend publication after a major revision whose experimental burden is small.

## Major points

1. Coefficients are the unsolved problem and must be faced directly. Exact path transport and least-squares projection of the exact field are oracles; the paper's own encoder test (Supplementary Fig. S32) sits 54 points below oracle coefficients. The Discussion never mentions that result. Fig. 5 offers a natural repair: in that task the proximal inhibition is the context, so a rule that delivers the somatic error only to the uninhibited subtree is local and implementable. Add it. If it succeeds the paper has a mechanism; if it fails, the abstract must say that routed-credit benefits were demonstrated with oracle coefficients.

2. Fig. 5 does not yet name the distinction it tests. In a directed passive tree all path gains are positive, so exact credit and broadcast differ only in the context-dependent gain ratio between the two subtrees; the required resolution is one bit, which subtree. Please add a two-profile dictionary (the two subtrees, no proximal pair) to show it suffices, show what the broadcast student converges to (I expect aligned tuning in both subtrees, which would explain the plateau at NMSE 0.63), and relate the cancellation in panel F to the compatibility argument of Fig. 2, since it is the same conflict. Also report the aligned-task numbers: broadcast reaches 4.6e-4 against 3.8e-7 for exact credit, a thousandfold ratio that "learned accurately" conceals on the log axis of panel C.

3. The anatomical target field favors ancestry by construction. Fig. 8 proves that a focal shunt's adjoint effect is piecewise constant on ancestry blocks, so the focal-shunt fields used as the "independent" target in Fig. 7 are sums of ancestry-block patterns. The text says the field is independent of dictionary selection, which is true, but not that it is structurally ancestry-shaped. The surrogate-tree control (advantage 0.041) is the honest comparison and should lead the paragraph, or add a second field generated by distributed synaptic perturbations rather than focal shunts.

4. Fixed budgets on both sides. All fifty D3 fits are still at the 600-epoch cap with falling loss, so the LocalCA accuracy reversal is itself a finite-budget outcome, and exact path retains lower cross-entropy. Run the LocalCA pair to a stopping criterion, or report where the curves cross. In Fig. 4C some exact quartic fits reach the floor while others stay near 0.6 at 1,024 steps; state this bimodality and test a longer budget.

5. Single dendritic layer with exact readout errors. This is now stated, but "one signal per neuron captures most of the benefit" is then a statement about within-arbor resolution downstream of an exact neuron error whose rank is at most ten under a linear readout. Either add one two-layer dendritic cohort or qualify the abstract sentence accordingly.

6. The measured-response null needs a sensitivity statement. With seven targets, report by simulation the partial correlation detectable under the observed missing-data pattern, and change "show no preferential ancestry alignment" to "provide no evidence of". State in the text that the restricted rules underperform a treeless ridge regression (0.83 versus 0.80 NMSE).

7. The pairwise anomaly in Fig. 4B is left open. The initial profile reaches the noise floor by step 200 while exact credit plateaus near 0.08 until step 512. Line 221 says Adam's scale invariance does not explain it; offer a hypothesis (a saddle in the product coefficients) or state it is unexplained, and note that it makes the primary contrast conservative by only 0.008.

8. Positioning. Legenstein and Maass 2011 is in the bibliography but not cited; Poirazi and Mel 2001, Jones and Kording 2021 and Beniaguev et al. 2021 are absent, although Figs. 2 to 5 use exactly their tree-structured single-neuron setting. One sentence should also list what is new relative to the predecessor preprint.

9. Length and structure. The narrative is about 6,100 words against 5,000 guidance, Methods about 3,100, with nine figures and a 145-page supplement. Fig. 3 (a 1.27-point effect predicted by the generator) could merge into Fig. 2, since both concern which branch receives the signal. Add a contents table to the supplement mapping each main figure to its sections and figures.

## Minor points

- Abstract: the third sentence is 44 words and carries four results; split it.
- The prospective-selector failure is reported at the end of the image section (line 136), where it is out of place; move it to Methods or a short paragraph of its own.
- Terminology: pattern, profile, route, column and address are used interchangeably; define once and hold.
- Equation 3 ends with a comma followed by a new sentence.
- Methods, conductance task: students inherit the teacher's nominal coupling and proximal gains (perturbed by 0.4 in log space) and start only the terminal gains at one; say so in the main Methods, since a referee could read "generic" initialization as uninformed.
- Fig. 5B: label the tuning curves as nominal teacher curves.
- Fig. 8D: add "negative = attenuation" in the panel.
- Fig. 9D: draw the ridge value as a reference line.
- Discussion: add the encoder gap (S32) and the oracle nature of the Fig. 4 and Fig. 5 coefficients.
- Data availability: the in-development proxy-label table is acceptable given that all principal findings are repeated without it; keep that sentence.
- Statistics, versions, fluorescence processing and hyperparameters are now adequately reported.

## Recommendation

Major revision. Points 1 to 4 and 6 require analyses on existing models; the remainder is text. The science underneath is sound and the reporting standard is well above the norm for the field.
