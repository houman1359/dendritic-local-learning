# Interaction structure and credit: completed manuscript revision

This revision replaces the original initialization-only morphology-selection claim with independently tested statements about representational capacity, estimation from noisy examples and subsequent learning. The Nature Communications manuscript remains the sole intended publication. No journal submission or external upload was performed.

## Strongest new evidence

- **A working combined procedure.** On 80 fresh functions in 20 paired seed blocks, trees estimated from 256 noisy calibration queries and trained with exact credit reached mean test NMSE 0.1246, versus 0.5837 for the development-selected fixed tree. The paired improvement was 0.4591, with a multiplicity-adjusted 97.5% interval [0.3849, 0.5323]. Broadcasting on estimated trees gave 0.7321; its excess over exact credit was 0.6075 [0.5692, 0.6461]. Both predeclared comparisons passed the 0.01 NMSE margin. Matching, quartet and nested targets reached approximately 0.024–0.027, near the 0.0225 noise floor. Random interactions retained 0.4229 error. Broadcast had slightly lower error on matching targets.
- **Useful prediction from finite noisy observations.** In a separate 20-seed cohort, the estimated interaction score achieved fixed-pool regret 0.0274 versus 0.1561 for the fixed tree and 0.0872 for a two-sweep pilot. Both adjusted primary comparisons passed. The estimator received a generic 255-feature sparse Fourier dictionary, without task-family labels or planted supports.
- **Rank alone does not specify structure.** All 105 matching and 35 quartet targets share the full input-sensitivity matrix I/4, while their compatibility with candidate groupings differs. A necessary and sufficient centered-cut criterion constructs exact scalar multi-affine trees. All 140 primary targets require minimum depth three; 24 nested controls require depth four. The nested controls have the same rank but a different spectrum. All 164 constructions reproduce targets to numerical precision. This is a model-specific theorem, not a theorem for general conductance neurons.
- **A physical-model bridge.** In positive-conductance trees with identical shape and resource count, exact-credit Adam attained NMSE 0.000150 for compatible input grouping versus 0.028921 for incompatible grouping. Missing orthogonal interaction components give an analytic population-error lower bound for the latter, evaluated by converged quadrature. Calibrated broadcast matched exact credit closely under Adam; the grouping result does not require a general exact-credit advantage.
- **Learning controls retain heterogeneity.** A separate 3,600-fit experiment learns oracle-compatible algebraic trees from label-independent initialization. Exact credit is valuable on quartet and nested targets, while restricted subtree profiles do not consistently outperform shuffled profiles. All learning rates, optimizer settings, input assignments and bounded-parameter diagnostics remain available.

The four new confirmatory experiments comprise 7,760 fits: 2,240 finite-calibration ALS fits, 480 combined estimation/gradient-learning fits, 3,600 credit-control fits and 1,440 conductance fits. Every scientific outcome is retained. Three documented pre-fit interface/dispatch repairs preserve original sources, amendment hashes and failed launch records; no outcome-driven retuning resulted. An excluded complete smoke run is separate from confirmation.

## Scope and validation

The estimated-tree pipeline assumes a known eight-variable Rademacher domain and a sparse Fourier dictionary whose size grows exponentially. Its bounded finite-horizon Adam recipes are fixed before confirmation. The target-informed tree is a privileged structure reference, not a guaranteed endpoint ceiling. Independent noisy test draws may repeat patterns from the finite input domain. Conductance results concern task-matched grouping within one directed architecture. Anatomical capacity and modeled transport still do not establish endogenous biological teaching signals.

Independent numerical audits reproduce every calibration choice, all 480 saved combined-learning endpoints, the conductance statistics and the exact-gradient identities. Primary protocols, selection seals, source hashes, runtime amendments, full outcomes and validation reports accompany the five morphology Source Data folders. Production-model, tree, estimator and gradient checks are retained with the code. Final manuscript/layout/inventory checks and archive fingerprints are recorded separately under `analysis/morphology_credit_revision_20260905/` to avoid circular archive hashes.
